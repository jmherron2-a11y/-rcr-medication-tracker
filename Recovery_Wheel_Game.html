🎡 Recovery Wheel of Fortune

Spin • Guess • Solve • Celebrate recovery skills

On your turn: spin, guess a consonant, or buy a vowel for $250 in your round bank. Correct consonants earn the wheel value for each occurrence.

Group facilitator tip

After each puzzle, invite the group to explain the phrase, share a coping example, or identify a recovery skill. Puzzles are designed for adults in substance-use treatment and are not diagnostic assessments.

Round bank is added to the team's total only when that team solves the puzzle. Bankrupt clears the current team's round bank, not prior winnings.
